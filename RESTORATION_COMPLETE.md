## 🚀 HOW TO USE

### **For Students (User Module):**

1. **First Time Users**:
   - Go to: `http://localhost/community/activate.php`
   - Enter: Register Number, DOB, Email, Phone
   - Click "Activate Account"

2. **Login**:
   - Go to: `http://localhost/community/login.php`
   - Enter: Register Number and Date of Birth
   - Click "Sign In"

3. **Dashboard Features**:
   - **Radar Tab**: View nearby emergency requests
   - **Profile Tab**: See your points, blood group, donation history
   - **SOS Tab**: Trigger emergency blood request

### **For Admin:**

1. **Login**:
   - Go to: `http://localhost/community/admin/`
   - Enter admin credentials

2. **Dashboard Features**:
   - Manage hospital blood stocks
   - View volunteer list
   - Monitor active alerts
   - Manage donation camps
   - Update student registry
